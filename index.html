About me

$ whoamicesarbeltran · cloud · forensics · research · teaching

I am an Electronic Engineer from Universidad Cooperativa de Colombia, hold a Specialization in Telematics Network Security from Universidad El Bosque, and earned a Master's in Digital Security from Pontificia Universidad Javeriana, where I graduated Cum Laude.

I have extensive experience in enterprise IT infrastructure, including Windows Server and Linux administration, virtualization with VMware, Hyper-V and Oracle VM, and cloud services on Microsoft Azure and Microsoft 365. I complement this experience with knowledge of penetration testing, ethical hacking, digital forensics, technology risk management and the ISO/IEC 27001 standard.

I currently work as an IT Cloud Engineer Specialist at Inchcape Digital, where I focus on cloud security: security controls, vulnerability management, and identity and access management. I am also a professor in the Faculty of Engineering at Universidad El Bosque, where I teach information security and ethical hacking.
